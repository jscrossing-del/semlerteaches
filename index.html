Reel Log App

The fishing log that actually helps you catch more fish

Reel Log lets you track every trip — species, bait, water temp, depth, weather, and location — so that over time you can see exactly what's working and when.

I built it because I kept forgetting the details that mattered. Now I never go back to the water without knowing what worked there before.

How it works

Log the details, spot the patterns

After each trip, log where you fished, what you caught, what bait worked, and the conditions. Wind, temp, pressure, water clarity — all of it.

Reel Log finds the patterns in your data so you know what to tie on before you even get to the lake.
